POSEIDON - THE GREEK GOD OF THE SEA

Facts

Poseidon is the Greek god of the sea, earthquakes, and horses. He is one of the Olympian gods and is often depicted holding a trident. After overthrowing the titans, Zeus, Poseidon, and Hades divided the world among themselves in which Poseidon took the seas. But Poseidon doesn't just rule the seas, he also rules other bodies of water like the ocean and springs. He lives in a palace under the sea. He is known for his temper which creates earthquakes, storms, and tsunamis. It is also believed he tamed horses and created them. He is associated with bulls and dolphins as well.

Relationships

Siblings: Hestia, Demeter, Hera, Hades, Zeus, Chiron(half-sibling). Parents: Cronus and Rhea. Children: Triton, Polyphemus, Theseus, Pegasus, Orion, Atlas, and many more. Spouse: Amphitrite, but it is said he took other partners as well.

Beliefs and goals

Poseidon values honor and power above all else. He believes that mortals owe him their sacrifice, fear, and worship. He believes that his authority is equal to Zeus's, the king of the gods. He often resents Zeus for this and have plotted to overthrow him, but failed. He is fiercely protective of his territories. Despite this, he is known to protect sea creatures aand sea nymphs who serve him, as well as his children who obey him. His goals are to maintain his power and authority over the seas, expand his influence, and avenge those who wrong him and his people.

Stories & important events

The Contest

There was a contest between Poseidon and Athena. The rule was to give the citizens of Greece a gift, and the one to give a more useful gift would be the winner. Poseidon gave the citizens a horse, but Athena gave them an olive tree. The citizens found the olive tree to be more useful, and Athena won the contest. Poseidon was enraged and flooded the city of Athens in retaliation, but Athena protected the city. It is also why the city is named Athens as a tribute to Athena.

The Curse on Odysseus

After Odysseus blinded his son, Polyphemus the cyclops, Poseidon cursed him and made his journey home to Ithaca extremely difficult by sending storms and violent waves at his ship.

Atlantis

According to legend, Atlantis was a powerful and advanced civilization created by Poseidon and ruled by his son, Atlas. Over time, the kings that ruled Atlantis became corrupt and greedy, angering the gods. As a result, Zeus gathered the gods and passed judgement, creating a tsunami that drowned the entire city and pulled it under the sea, leading to its destruction and disappearance from history.

Trivia

Poseidon is responsible for the creation of the first horse. His Roman counterpart is Neptune. He was called the "Earth-Shaker" because he can cause earthquakes using his trident. The brothers, Hades, Zeus, and Poseidon, diviided the world by drawing lots.
