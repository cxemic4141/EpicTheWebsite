ATHENA

THE GREEK GODDESS OF WISDOM

Facts

Athena is the Greek goddess of wisdom, courage, and warfare. She is known for her strategic thinking and is often depicted with an owl, symbolizing wisdom. She is a virgin goddess and is associated with the city of Athens, which was named in her honor. Athena played a key role in many Greek myths, including the Odyssey and the Iliad, where she provided guidance and support to heroes like Odysseus and Achilles.

Relationships

  • Zeus: Father
  • Metis: Mother
  • Ares: Half Brother
  • Poseidon: Rival
  • Odysseus: Favored Hero

Beliefs and Goals

Athena valued wisdom, strategy, and justice. She believed that careful planning is more effective than reckless violence. She also supported heroes who demonstrated courage, intelligence and resourcefulness. She protected Athens and its people and valued skill, craftmanship and knowledge.

Stories & Important Events

Birth

Athena was born fully grown and armored from the forehead of her father, Zeus, which was the cause of all his unbearable headaches.

The Olive Tree

Athena and Poseidon competed to become the patron deity of a city. Poseidon offered saltwater spring or in other versions, a horse. Athena gifted an olive tree, which rperesented peace and prosperity. The people chose Athena's gift and the city was named Athens in her honor.

The Odyssey

Athena supported Odysseus throughout his journey home from the Trojan War, using her wisdom and strategic thinking to help him overcome various challenges.

Trivia

  • She is immune to Aphrodite's powers.
  • Her sacred animal is the owl, that represents wisdom.
  • She was knowm as a virgin goddess and did not marry.
  • Whenever Ares and her would clash, she would always win because of her strategic thinking and intelligence.
